Arranging numbers from 1 to 10
Numbers from 1 to 10 can be arranged in order.
Example 1
Arrange the following numbers from the smallest to the largest.
1 3 6 2 5 4
Answer
1 2 3 4 5 6
Example 2
Arrange the following numbers from the largest to the smallest.
8 5 10 9 6 4 7
Answer
10 9 8 7 6 5 4
