Adding numbers vertically without regrouping

Example
Add vertically.
  43
+ 21
Solution
  43
+ 21
64
Steps
1.Add the ones: 3 + 1 = 4. Write 4 in the ones place of the answer. 
  43
+ 21
────
   4
2.Add the tens: 4 + 2 = 6. Write 6 in the tens place of the answer.

Therefore, the answer is 64.

+ 21
