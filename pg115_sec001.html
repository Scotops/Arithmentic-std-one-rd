Subtracting numbers vertically by regrouping

Numbers can be subtracted vertically by regrouping tens into ones.

Example
72
− 38
Solution
72
− 38
34

Steps

1.Subtract the ones: 2 − 8, is not possible.
72
− 38
2.Take 1 ten from the group of 7 tens.Change it into 10 ones.Add the ones, 10 + 2 = 12.Remember 6 tens remained.
6 12
72
− 38
3.Now, subtract the ones: 12 − 8 = 4.Write 4 in the ones place of the answer.
6 12
72
− 38
4
