Subtracting numbers horizontally by regrouping

Numbers can be subtracted horizontally by regrouping tens into ones.

Example 1

Solution
23 − 19 = 4
Steps
1. Subtract the ones: 3 − 9, is not possible.

2. Take 1 ten from the group of 2 tens. Change it into 10 ones. Add the 10 ones to the 3 ones to make 13 ones.

3. Subtract the ones: 13 − 9 = 4
13 − 9 = 4
4. Subtract the tens: 1 − 1 = 0, so the answer is 4.
